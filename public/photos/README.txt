PUT YOUR PHOTOS HERE
--------------------
Copy your photos into this folder, then reference them in src/config.js
WITHOUT a leading slash, e.g.

  photo: 'photos/us-1.jpg'

Tips
- Resize to ~1600px on the long edge and compress (squoosh.app or tinypng.com).
  Aim for under 300 KB each so the page opens fast on mobile data.
- JPG or WebP are best. Portrait photos work best in the arch frame.
